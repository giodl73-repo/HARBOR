# Longshore Worker

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures the dockworkers who actually work the cargo.

## Primary concerns

- Jobs, safety, and the terms of automation (DIM-09).
- Working conditions and throughput pressure (DIM-09/03).
- Whether productivity gains are shared.

## Used during

- Labor/automation scoring; tier/SLA feasibility review with the port-authority/alliance realist.
