# Shipper / Beneficial Cargo Owner

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures the importer/exporter whose cargo moves through the port.

## Primary concerns

- Reliable, predictable transit and low dwell (DIM-03/05).
- Total landed cost incl. drayage and inland (DIM-08/12).
- Resilience against port disruption (DIM-05).

## Used during

- Dwell, resilience, and intermodal scoring; tier/SLA for gateway/national ports (T1/T2).
