# Port-Adjacent Community

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures the fenceline and drayage-corridor communities, often
environmental-justice neighborhoods.

## Primary concerns

- Air quality from port and truck emissions (DIM-10).
- Truck traffic, noise, and safety on local streets (DIM-10/11).
- Whether any economic benefit reaches the community (DIM-11).

## Used during

- Environmental and equity scoring; design-proposal siting review with the environmental
  advocate.
